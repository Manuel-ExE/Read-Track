Frequently Asked Questions

Why do you need camera permission?
We capture a single verification photo at the start of each session to ensure the reading session is authentic. The photo is never stored permanently and is only used for verification purposes.
Why do you need location permission?
Location data helps verify where reading sessions take place. This is especially useful for schools and organizations tracking reading assignments.
Is my data secure?
Yes. All data is transmitted over HTTPS, validated on the server, and only sent to your authorized Telegram channel. We never sell or share your data with third parties.
Can I use this offline?
The app works offline for reading, but session verification and reports require an internet connection to send data to Telegram.

Terms & Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

Welcome to ReadTrack. By using this service you agree to these terms.

2. Data We Collect

During a reading session we collect:

  • Location data (latitude, longitude, GPS accuracy)
  • Camera photo (single verification photo)
  • Session metadata (start time, end time, duration, device info)

3. How We Use Your Data

All data is used solely for verifying legitimate reading sessions and is forwarded to your authorized Telegram channel.

4. Data Retention

Session records are retained for up to 90 days unless required by law.

5. User Consent

By tapping "I Agree & Continue" you give explicit consent for data collection as described above.
