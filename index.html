Frequently Asked Questions

Why do you need camera permission?
We capture a single verification photo at the start of each session. The photo is only used for verification purposes.
Why do you need location permission?
Location data helps verify where reading sessions take place, especially useful for schools tracking assignments.
Is my data secure?
Yes. All data is transmitted over HTTPS, validated on the server, and only sent to your authorized Telegram channel.
Can I use this offline?
The app works offline for reading, but session verification and community features require an internet connection.
