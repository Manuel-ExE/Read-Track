Terms & Privacy Policy

Terms & Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

Welcome to ReadTrack. By using this service you agree to these terms. Please read them carefully before starting a reading session.

2. Data We Collect

During a reading session we collect:

  • Location data — latitude, longitude, and GPS accuracy.
  • Camera photo — a single verification photo taken with your front camera.
  • Session metadata — start time, end time, duration, device type, browser, and operating system.

3. How We Use Your Data

All collected data is used solely for verifying that a legitimate reading session was completed. Data is transmitted securely to our backend and forwarded to an authorized Telegram channel for record-keeping.

4. Camera Permission

We request access to your front-facing camera to take a single verification photo at session start. The camera stream is never recorded continuously; only one still image is captured.

5. Location Permission

We request your device location to verify where the reading session took place. Location data is captured once at session start and once at session end.

6. Data Retention

Session records are retained for up to 90 days and then permanently deleted unless required by applicable law.

7. Third-Party Services

We use the Telegram API to deliver session reports. No data is sold or shared with any other third party.

8. User Consent

By tapping "I Agree & Continue" you give explicit informed consent for the collection and processing described above.

9. Contact

For any questions regarding your data, contact us through the Telegram channel linked to your reading programme.
